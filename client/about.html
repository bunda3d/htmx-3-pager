About this demo

This proof of concept explores a practical question: how much interactivity does a small website need, and how simply can it be built?

🎯 A good fit

This approach may suit a small business, project, or product site that needs a few pages, a contact form, and a modest amount of saved data.

🧩 Why this stack

PHP handles requests, htmx loads HTML fragments, and SQLite stores submissions in a database file. This keeps the demo’s structure compact and avoids a frontend build step.

💰 Cost and maintenance

PHP and SQLite are available with many hosting setups, which can keep deployment needs modest. Actual cost depends on hosting, traffic, backups, and support requirements.

⚖️ Trade-offs

htmx exchanges HTML rather than JSON, and SQLite is intended here for modest workloads. A real deployment also needs protected access to submitted records, backups, and appropriate data handling.
